    Game Rules

    General Rules

    • Each player starts with two live hands showing 1 chopstick each.
    • On your turn, choose one action: attack an opponent hand or split your own chopsticks between your two hands.
    • When you attack, your chosen hand adds its chopstick count to the opponent hand you tap.
    • A hand is knocked out when it reaches the mode's knockout number.
    • You win when both opposing hands are knocked out.
    • Splits must keep the same total number of chopsticks across your two hands.
    • You cannot split into the exact same hand values or simply inverse them, such as changing 3-1 into 1-3.
    • Each turn has a 30 second timer. If it expires before the match timer, that player forfeits and the other player wins.
    • Every mode also has a 10-minute Match Timer, shown at the top right. It starts with the match and does not reset between turns, on reconnect, or when reopening a saved game.
    • If the Match Timer reaches 0 first, both players draw. Draws grant no EXP, coins, rewards, or trophies, and do not change trophy totals or win/loss streaks. If both timers expire together, the match is a draw.
    • Win streaks are tracked separately for Play vs AI difficulties and Play vs Friends modes.

    Standard Mode

    • Hands are knocked out at 5 or more chopsticks.
    • Splits can move chopsticks between your two hands as long as the split is legal.

    Play on Same Device

    • Two players share one device and pass it back and forth after each turn.
    • No EXP, coins, achievements, quests, or win streaks are awarded in this mode.
    • This mode is meant for local play or practice with oneself.

    Play vs AI

    • You play against a bot using Standard Mode rules.
    • Higher AI difficulties grant better rewards.
    • Easy AI matches do not count toward achievements, but they still count toward quests.

    Play vs Friends

    • Invite a friend and play on separate devices.
    • Standard Play vs Friends keeps its own win streak.

    Ranked Mode

    • Ranked uses Standard rules. Join the matchmaking queue to find an opponent; friend invites are for Standard games.
    • Everyone begins at Dirt with 0 trophies. Ranks are Dirt, Plain, Vanilla, Matcha, and Divine.
    • Reach 100 trophies within a rank to advance, carrying extra trophies forward. A loss that reaches 0 demotes you to the previous rank, carrying any deficit. Dirt cannot fall below 0. Divine has no upper limit.
    • Win / loss ranges: Dirt +15–21 / −7–10; Plain +13–19 / −9–13; Vanilla +11–17 / −11–16; Matcha +11–17 / −13–18; Divine +10–15 / −15–20.
    • Opponent strength uses overall trophies and win rates, with small samples weighted less. Stronger opponents mean bigger win gains and smaller losses. Your first 20 Ranked games give a small win bonus within these ranges. Values are fixed from stats at match start.
    • Forfeits and expired turn timers count as losses. Final moves finish the match immediately.
    • View your rank on Profile. Open Leaderboard from Home to see players in your rank or choose another rank. Players are sorted by trophies, then username for ties.

    Monthly Seasons

    • Seasons run from the first day of each month at 00:00 UTC to the start of the next month. A match finishing across the boundary counts toward the month it started.
    • Play at least one Ranked game that season to receive one reward for your highest rank reached: Dirt 100 gold + 1 Chai Special; Plain 250 + 2; Vanilla 500 + 3; Matcha 800 + 4; Divine 1,200 + 5. Rewards are added automatically after season settlement.
    • The season reset reduces overall trophies by 0% in Dirt, 10% in Plain, 15% in Vanilla, 20% in Matcha, and 25% in Divine, based on your ending rank. Remaining trophies are rounded to the nearest whole number and determine your new rank.
    • Overall trophies = rank starting total (0 / 100 / 200 / 300 / 400) + trophies within that rank. For example, Divine 45 is 445 overall; a 25% reduction leaves 334, or Matcha 34.
